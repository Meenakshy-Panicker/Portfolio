About

Every project I take on starts from the same question: how do I build technology that survives contact with resource-constrained, high-stakes, real-world systems and leaves them stronger, not just more automated.

The logo, മ (Ma), is more than a letter from the Malayalam alphabet. Long before it became my logo, it was the beginning of my story.

It reminds me of my Mā (mother), who gave me both my name, Meenakshy, and my roots as a Malayali. As I grew, the same letter came to represent the values I wanted to carry with me, especially Manushyatham (humanity).

Today, whenever I see മ, I'm reminded that the pursuit of Manoharam (beauty) begins there.

01 · Selected Projects

InstaFarm

AI-powered B2B procurement platform for agricultural supply chains

Role
Founder & CEO
Domain
Agricultural Supply Chain
Stack
Price prediction (ML), demand forecasting (time-series), CNN crop grading, agentic AI matching

Overview

I built InstaFarm as a full-stack AI platform that automates crop sourcing and supply-chain transparency for B2B agricultural procurement, replacing fragmented, manual trading relationships with price-prediction, demand-forecasting, and computer-vision quality grading.

Problem

I saw that smallholder and mid-size farms routinely lose margin to intermediaries and unpredictable demand, while buyers struggle with inconsistent supply and limited visibility into quality or provenance.

Solution

I built a price-prediction and demand-forecasting pipeline, fine-tuned on APMC market data, weather APIs, and festival calendars, paired with a CNN-based crop-quality grading module that replaces manual inspection at FPO collection points.

Impact

I reduced post-harvest price uncertainty by an estimated 20–30% for participating farmers, partnered with 6 Farmer Producer Organisations, and onboarded 80+ women farmers across 4 districts in Kerala and Tamil Nadu. I was recognised with the ADB CAREC Climate Resilience Challenge, Asia-Pacific (2025–26).

Technology

I used price-prediction and time-series demand-forecasting models, a CNN-based computer-vision grading module, and agentic AI for supplier matching.

Hardware Engineer
Domain
Water Security

Overview

I built a thermoelectric dehumidifier that extracts drinkable water from ambient humidity, designed for affordability and field reliability, to serve underserved communities and street animals during dry months.

02 · Selected Projects

Price Forecasting

Agricultural Commodity Price Forecasting: ML Benchmarking Study

Role
ML Research Engineer
Domain
Agricultural Commodity Markets / Time-Series Forecasting
Stack
ARIMA, SARIMA, ETS, XGBoost, LightGBM, Prophet, Python
Status
Manuscript under review for journal publication

Overview

I built a modular, testable forecasting codebase for agricultural commodity prices, designed to extend cleanly to new commodities or data sources with minimal friction.

Problem

I noticed that comparing forecasting approaches for commodity prices typically means one-off scripts per model family, making fair comparison, extension to new commodities, and reproducibility difficult.

Solution

I built a consistent evaluation protocol benchmarking statistical baselines (ARIMA, SARIMA, ETS) against gradient-boosted trees (XGBoost, LightGBM) and a neural/hybrid model (Prophet), backed by type-hinted, logged, config-driven code and automated tests.

Impact

I produced publication-quality visualisations and summary tables suitable for a research paper, while demonstrating software-engineering best practices (type hints, logging, config management, automated tests) in an ML research context. My accompanying manuscript is currently under review for journal publication.

Technology

I used statistical time-series models (ARIMA/SARIMA/ETS), gradient-boosted trees (XGBoost, LightGBM), Prophet, and a config-driven, tested Python codebase.
